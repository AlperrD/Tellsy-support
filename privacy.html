Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tellsy handles information when you record, use the teleprompter and manage a Tellsy Pro subscription. Tellsy is designed to keep your scripts and recordings on your device.

Effective: 19 November 2026 /Tellsy: Dual Cam Teleprompter

Tellsy does not upload your videos, audio or scripts to our servers. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.

1. Scope and controller

This policy applies to the Tellsy mobile application (“Tellsy”, “the app”) and to support communications. The developer and data controller is Alper Duman. Privacy questions can be sent to yadateknolojiofficial@gmail.com.

Tellsy is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to App Store services, platform data and payment processing.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use Tellsy, the following information may be involved:

  • Scripts you write or paste into the app. Scripts are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded by Tellsy.
  • Camera and microphone content while you record, and the recordings you create. Recording and teleprompter processing happen on your device.
  • Recordings you choose to save to your photo library, subject to your device and iCloud settings.
  • Subscription information received from our subscription management provider: purchase history, product and entitlement status, an anonymous app user ID, and limited device, platform, country and currency information.
  • Support communications: your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send.
  • Local app preferences such as teleprompter speed, text size and appearance settings.

Tellsy does not collect advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, health data or browsing history, and does not use your content for advertising.

3. Camera, microphone and photo permissions

Camera access is required to record video and to show the dual-camera preview. Microphone access is required to capture audio. Photo library access is used only when you choose to save a recording or import media.

Permissions are controlled by iOS. You can review or change them at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security. Some features will not work without the relevant permission.

4. On-device processing and storage

Tellsy records, previews and scrolls your script on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your video, audio or scripts, and we do not provide cloud synchronization for them.

If you share a recording or export it to another app, the destination service’s privacy policy applies. Your device backup settings, such as iCloud Backup, may also include app data; those backups are controlled by you and Apple.

5. Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Tellsy Pro is an auto-renewing subscription purchased through the Apple App Store. Apple processes your payment and does not share your full card details with us.

We use RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to validate purchases, unlock Pro features and restore purchases. RevenueCat may receive a purchase receipt, product identifier, entitlement status, an anonymous app user ID, and limited device, platform, country and currency information. RevenueCat acts as our service provider and processes this information to provide subscription management. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

We do not receive or store your payment card number, billing address or Apple Account password.

6. Third parties, sharing and tracking

The only third-party service used for app functionality is our subscription management provider, together with Apple’s App Store and payment infrastructure. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Tellsy does not include third-party advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. Because we do not track you, Tellsy does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety, and in connection with a business transfer where permitted.

7. Security, retention and deletion

Scripts and recordings remain on your device under your control. Protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS up to date. No system is completely risk-free.

You can delete recordings from Photos, delete scripts inside Tellsy, and remove local app data by deleting the app. Subscription records are retained by Apple and our subscription management provider according to their policies and legal obligations. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and meet legal requirements.

To request access, correction or deletion of personal information we control, email yadateknolojiofficial@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request; please do not send recordings or identity documents unless asked.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of your data, or withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because Tellsy keeps your media on your device and uses a subscription management provider, some requests may need to be directed to Apple or that provider. We will help where we can.

9. Children and international use

Tellsy is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will address it.

Tellsy is available internationally. Apple and our subscription management provider may process subscription-related information in countries other than yours, using the safeguards described in their policies. Your scripts and recordings stay on your device.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published here with a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated where required.

Questions about privacy? Email yadateknolojiofficial@gmail.com.
