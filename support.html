Frequently asked questions

Recording & teleprompter

How does dual-camera recording work?

Open the camera screen, choose a dual-camera layout and grant camera and microphone access. Tellsy shows the rear camera as your main view and the front camera as a picture-in-picture inset. Tap record to capture both perspectives in one take.

How do I control the teleprompter?

Add or paste a script, open the teleprompter and start scrolling. You can change speed and text size, pause or resume, and enable mirror mode when you use a beam-splitter rig. Test your settings before recording.

Where are my recordings saved?

Finished recordings are saved to your photo library on your device when you tap save. Tellsy does not upload them. If a save fails, check Photos permission and available storage.

Why is dual camera not available?

Simultaneous cameras depend on your device hardware and iOS version. Close other camera apps, restart Tellsy and make sure iOS is up to date. If the option is still missing, your device may not support that combination.

The teleprompter text is hard to read.

Increase the text size, slow the scroll speed, raise screen brightness and reduce glare. Use mirror mode only with a compatible rig; otherwise keep it off. Place the phone close to the lens so your eyeline stays natural.

Tellsy Pro & billing

How do I restore Tellsy Pro?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. Open the Tellsy Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. If Pro still does not appear, email us with the purchase date and the Apple receipt — never send card details.

How do I cancel Tellsy Pro?

On iOS, open Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions > Tellsy and choose Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period.

Why was I charged after canceling?

Cancellation stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period automatically. Check the renewal date in your App Store subscription settings. Refund requests are handled by Apple.

Privacy & data

Does Tellsy upload my videos or scripts?

No. Recording, scripts and teleprompter processing stay on your device. Subscription management does not receive your videos, audio or scripts.

How do I delete my data?

Delete recordings from Photos, delete scripts inside Tellsy, and delete the app to remove local app data. For privacy requests about information we control, email us.

Do I need an account?

No. Tellsy does not require an account. Subscription entitlements are linked anonymously to your installation.

Still stuck?

Email us with your device model, iOS version, Tellsy version and what happened. Please do not attach private recordings or identity documents.
